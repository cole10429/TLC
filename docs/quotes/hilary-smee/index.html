Terms & Conditions

  1. Upon accepting quote, Tender Loving Carpentry will confirm a start date, and will request the material cost of job. Upon completion of section of work a labour draw can be requested for that section of work.
  2. Disposal costs include a $1,500 waste allowance for the overall project. Any disposal costs exceeding this allowance shall be billed additionally to the homeowner at cost.
  3. Existing bifold door locations may contain visible screw holes or hardware marks within existing jambs and trim after removal. Repair or replacement of jamb material beyond included scope shall be additional if requested.
  4. TLC has included pricing for interior door hardware, cabinet pulls, cabinet handles, and cabinet hinges as specified. These items may be removed from contract pricing if homeowner elects to supply.
  5. Tile installation pricing is based on a standard tile layout and pattern. Specialty layouts including herringbone, intricate patterns, mosaics, feature strips, or accent installations shall be priced separately.
  6. TLC will attempt to achieve flush flooring transitions where possible. Transition strips or reducers may be required depending on material thickness and shall be additional if required.
  7. Quote assumes existing subfloor conditions are structurally sound and within acceptable tolerances for tile \ floor installation. Additional floor preparation, self-leveling, or subfloor repair beyond standard preparation shall be additional if required.
  8. Quote assumes existing framing, plumbing rough-ins, and concealed conditions are in serviceable condition. Any concealed damage, rot, mold, structural deficiencies, or unforeseen repairs discovered during demolition shall be billed separately upon homeowner approval.
  9. Window installation pricing includes framing new window opening, installation of supplied window, waterproofing, insulation, and standard trim work only. Any siding replacement, repair, exterior finishing, or additional weather barrier work beyond reuse of existing materials shall be additional if required.
  10. Painting is limited to renovated areas specifically outlined within quote scope.
  11. Exclusions include countertops, carpeting, and electrical work unless otherwise specified.
  12. Any work outside the scope outlined above shall be treated as additional work and billed separately upon homeowner approval.
